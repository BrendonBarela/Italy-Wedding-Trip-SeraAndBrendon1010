While we're here

Art, opera & Italy's food heartland

Parma rewards a low-key itinerary: the Duomo district, Pilotta, and plenty of time for prosciutto, Parmigiano and fresh pasta.

1

Parma Cathedral

Romanesque cathedral with Correggio's dramatic dome fresco.

2

Baptistery of Parma

Pink Verona-marble landmark beside the cathedral.

3

Palazzo della Pilotta

Museums, National Gallery and the remarkable Teatro Farnese.

4

Teatro Regio

Historic opera house in a city with a deep music tradition.

Quick trip notes
