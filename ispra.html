Getting around Ispra

The rental car will be the easiest option

Ispra is much less transit-oriented than Verona or Parma. For the wedding weekend, plan on the rental car for groceries, nearby towns and most outings; use the lakefront on foot when staying local.

Best overall

🚗 Rental car

Most flexible for the villas, errands, Santa Caterina del Sasso and nearby lake towns.

In town

🚶 Walk

Best for the Ispra lakefront, harbor and a relaxed evening close to the villa.

When running

⛴️ Ferry

Ispra has a lake landing, but October service is seasonal. Check the official timetable before relying on it.
