While we're here

Romance, Roman history & easy evenings

Verona is best enjoyed slowly: one major sight, a long lunch, and an evening wandering the historic center.

1

Arena di Verona

Roman amphitheater and Verona's signature landmark.

2

Piazza delle Erbe

Historic market square—great for an easy wander and aperitivo.

3

Castelvecchio

Medieval castle, museum and photogenic bridge over the Adige.

4

Ponte Pietra

Beautiful Roman-era bridge and riverside views.

Good to know

Quick trip notes

  • Main rail hub: Verona Porta Nuova
  • Historic center is compact and very walkable
  • October: bring a light layer for evenings
