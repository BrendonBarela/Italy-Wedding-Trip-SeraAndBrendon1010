Before choosing your bags

Cabin bag: 55 × 40 × 23 cm (about 21.5 × 15.5 × 9 in), max 8 kg / 17.6 lb. Personal item: 40 × 30 × 15 cm. Our tickets include one checked suitcase each, max 23 kg / 50 lb and 62 linear inches / 157 cm total dimensions.

Pack light on purpose

Capsule plan

Built around one checked suitcase each (up to 50 lb / 23 kg on the booked flights), plus carry-on/personal items. The frequent laundry resets are what let us keep the clothing count small and leave room for wedding clothes and anything we buy.

Sera

  • 5 everyday underwear + 4–5 sock/tight pairs
  • 4 everyday tops + 3 bottoms
  • 1 cardigan/light sweater + 1 packable rain shell
  • 1 nicer dinner outfit
  • Walking shoes + one nicer pair
  • Wedding attire packed separately/protected
  • Sleepwear + daily toiletries/meds

Brendon

  • 5 underwear + 5 sock pairs
  • 4 everyday shirts + 2–3 pants
  • 1 light sweater/overshirt + 1 packable rain shell
  • 1 smart-casual dinner shirt
  • Walking shoes + one nicer pair
  • Wedding suit/outfit packed separately/protected
  • Sleepwear + daily toiletries/meds

Shared

  • 1 compact umbrella
  • 2 small day bags / personal items
  • Power bank + charging kit
  • Travel-size laundry detergent sheets
  • Small stain-removal pen
  • Document pouch for passports/tickets
  • Leave extra space for purchases
