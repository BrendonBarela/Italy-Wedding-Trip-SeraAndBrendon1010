Italian cheat sheet for the harder ingredients

Celeriac = sedano rapa · Swiss chard = bietole · leeks = porri · peas = piselli · fennel = finocchio · blackberries = more · chestnuts = castagne · beef for braising = manzo da brasato · cured meats = salumi · breadsticks = grissini.

Useful phrase: “Sto preparando uno stracotto per undici persone. Quale taglio di manzo mi consiglia?”
“I'm making stracotto for eleven people. Which cut of beef do you recommend?”

Best helper setup: Megan + one dedicated shopping helper on Oct. 8. Megan chooses ingredients; the helper handles cart, bags, loading, navigation and any second-stop run. That keeps Megan's attention on the food instead of logistics.

Music cues & rehearsal

Sierra officiates and runs the music. These playback lengths are suggested; rehearse with the exact recordings.

  1. Guests gather · Full song
    Linda Ronstadt — “I Love You for Sentimental Reasons.” Have extra gentle music ready if needed.
  2. Entrance · 45–90 seconds
    Instrumental “La Vie en Rose.” Start when Sera and Mike are ready. Fade once Sera is in position; follow the walk, not a stopwatch.
  3. Vows & rings · Music off
    Alex passes the rings when Sierra asks.
  4. Pronouncement & exit · About 90 seconds
    “This Will Be (An Everlasting Love).” Start immediately after the pronouncement; let it continue if the celebration is still flowing.
  5. First dance · About 2½ minutes
    Elton John — “Your Song.” Champagne first; start when the couple is ready. Fade at a natural musical pause.

Use a gentle 3–5-second fade. Download the songs, charge the speaker, silence notifications and keep the phone or remote within Sierra’s reach.
