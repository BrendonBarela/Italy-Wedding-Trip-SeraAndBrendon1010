Music & rehearsal cues

Sierra officiates and controls the music. Practice speaking and starting or fading each song from the ceremony spot at rehearsal. Download the exact recordings, charge the speaker, test the connection and silence notifications.

  1. Guests gather: Linda Ronstadt — “I Love You for Sentimental Reasons.”
  2. Sera’s entrance: Fade the gathering song and start the instrumental “La Vie en Rose” when Sera and her dad are ready. Fade out when they reach the front.
  3. Vows & rings: Music off. Alex hands over the rings when Sierra asks.
  4. Pronouncement & exit: Start “This Will Be (An Everlasting Love)” immediately after Sierra’s pronouncement. Practice the exact cue, kiss and exit.
  5. Champagne & first dance: Cindy pours first. When the couple is ready, Sierra starts Elton John’s “Your Song,” before Marta finishes at 7:00 PM.

At rehearsal, walk through everyone’s positions, the entrance, ring handoff and exit. Use placeholders for private vows.

Small things that will make the day easier

Suggested preparations — these are reminders, not extra scheduled events.

  • Before Marta arrives: Eat lunch, drink water, and put rings, vows, flowers and any details you want photographed in one place.
  • Music without juggling: Sierra should have the four tracks in order, downloaded, with the phone or remote on a small stand within reach. Test the speaker and Do Not Disturb, and rehearse the pronouncement-to-song transition.
  • A short photo list: Give Anna and Marta the must-have group combinations in advance. Let guests know to stay close until their photos are finished.
  • Champagne for everyone: Cindy can have water and an alcohol-free option ready alongside the glasses and chilled champagne.
  • A small comfort kit: Keep tissues, safety pins, stain wipes, touch-up items, a charger and comfortable shoes at the villa.
  • Keep the ceremony clear: Consider asking guests to put phones away during the ceremony so Marta has a clear view.
  • A quiet moment together: If the photo schedule allows, take a few minutes just for the two of you before dinner.
