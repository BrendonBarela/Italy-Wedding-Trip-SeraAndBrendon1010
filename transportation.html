Bag sizes & allowances · Checked September 21, 2026

What bags can we bring?

Flight allowances below are for Sera and Brendon’s booked Economy itinerary. Guests on different bookings should check their own tickets. Measure the packed bag including wheels, handles, and outer pockets. Centimeters are the reference; inch conversions are approximate.

Pack once for all our flights: one cabin bag no larger than 55 × 40 × 23 cm (about 21.5 × 15.5 × 9 in), weighing no more than 8 kg / 17.6 lb, plus one personal item within 40 × 30 × 15 cm (about 15.7 × 11.8 × 5.9 in). This uses the smallest limits across our operating airlines.

Ordinary luggage travels free. The standard baggage clause gives no fixed centimeter or kilogram allowance: bags must be easily carried and fit in the designated luggage spaces without blocking people or staff.

Italian regional trains · Including Oct 14

Trenitalia Regionale / RV

Ordinary hand-carried luggage is free. The published rule does not specify a numerical size or weight cap; use the dedicated spaces and keep aisles, doors, and other seats clear. Applies to Regionale 12356 and RV 3368; check the operator when choosing the flexible Verona–Parma train.

ZOU! / SNCF TER

No fixed luggage dimensions. You must handle your bags yourself and stow them in the designated spaces without endangering others. Label bags with your name and contact details. This covers the Ventimiglia–Beaulieu TER and local TER journeys.

Oct 7 & 11 · Milan ↔ MXP

Malpensa Express / Trenord

A numerical baggage allowance has not been verified for this airport service. Do not assume Trenitalia’s rules apply to Trenord. Check the current conditions before bringing unusually large or extra bags.

Malpensa Express conditions →

Wedding clothes: a garment bag counts toward your cabin allowance. Lufthansa permits a folding garment bag up to 57 × 54 × 15 cm and 8 kg as the carry-on, but do not assume that exception carries over to Air Dolomiti or Air Canada. A bag within the standard cabin dimensions is the simplest option across all flights. Train transfers: choose bags you can lift onto the train and manage together; airline acceptance does not guarantee easy train storage.

Local transportation cheat sheet

The decisions we don't want to research again on vacation

Ispra

🚗 Cars first

  • Villa Eden lists covered parking for up to four cars.
  • Use one car for people and one flexibly for supplies if we have two.
  • MXP ↔ Ispra: allow roughly 35–45 minutes by car or prebooked transfer.
  • Without a car: MXP → Gallarate → Sesto Calende by train, then a prearranged taxi to Ispra. Allow roughly 1–1½ hours including connections; check the exact itinerary.
  • Ispra station is served by replacement buses on the Sesto Calende route, with large gaps; do not assume a direct passenger train or frequent airport connection.
  • Prebook taxis for dinners when nobody wants to drive.
  • Park outside waterfront ZTL/restricted sections and walk the lakefront.
  • Santa Caterina: drive; the September free shuttle is not confirmed for our dates.
  • Ispra public lake service currently isn't something to rely on after Oct. 4.
