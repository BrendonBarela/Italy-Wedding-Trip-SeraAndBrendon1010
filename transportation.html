Why some buttons use Trainline instead of Omio

Omio can pre-apply departure dates through its affiliate search widget, but that widget requires a valid Omio / Impact affiliate partner ID. Omio's ordinary public route links reliably preserve the route, but its public documentation does not provide a stable date parameter for a normal link. Trainline does document date-and-time deep links, so the primary search buttons now open the route with our actual travel date and target departure time already filled in. Omio remains available as a secondary comparison where useful.
